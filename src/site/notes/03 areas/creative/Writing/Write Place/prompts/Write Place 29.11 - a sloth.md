# story
A lovely town. Nothing more than 20 thousand residents.
Life in this town was as slow or as calm as it could be.

Farmers arriving there from small villages trying to sell milk, eggs and other village goods on a bazaar.
Some people happily lived selling products at grocery stores, working in libraries and construction. It wasn’t anything exciting, but there was no rush.
Some people thought about how to escape from that town to the cities, or bigger, explore the world.

Everything here lived a happy slow life.

You could see some uncollected thrash once in a while. A garbage collector’s mistake? They forgot or overlooked collecting them?

No matter. There’s always next time. Nobody’s in the rush, albeit it ruins the scenery a little. 

Next street, on the part of the road disabled from active driving, an uncollected car was left alone for multiple weeks. Another deduct from the scenery scoring.

Another month a house set on fire, the firefighting crew arrived, swiftly extinguished the fire, didn’t take much longer than an hour.
But they left on foot, not collecting the ropes, the equipment or the truck.

Eventually they will. No rush.

This all didn’t seem urgent at first, at first a small overlook, but eventually the overlooks stacked and grew into a problems. Or a big problem that complicated the ordinary life in the town. 
Even a source of demotivation, as if they were emanating with a negative aura which ingrained a negative behavior.
To build an uncared place together, or to relax and slow down even more in this calm town.

Over the next months people put less and less effort into the neighborhood.

Streets filled with abandoned cars, fallen trees, soccer balls, toys.
Parks and playgrounds unfunctional due to accumulated thrash.
Schools stopped working as neither teachers nor students showed up.
Train station disabled due to unserviced rail.

This weird plague was taking over the town.
Everyone stayed home, some even died in isolation. Did they pick up the phone to call for help? No one knows, but even if they did. Would anyone actually come or even answer.

The town slowly became a ghost town.

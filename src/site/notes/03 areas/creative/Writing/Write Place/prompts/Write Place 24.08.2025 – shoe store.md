# story
Greg had to do it again, he had to finally get new shoes.
HE was such a miser. He walked in every pair of shoes until they were completely unusable. Those he had currently wearing. Who knew how many holes they had in them.
And he didn’t like to try and find new shoes. Period. But it was the time since he hated the feeling of his socks soaking wet. the weather ended up being quite rainy where he lived recently.

– “the store… wonderful store… too many freaking options” – he cussed under his nose walking in not noticing the shop assistant greeting him.
he looked at the shoes displayed – let’s just try these… – he chose the first pair that looked remotely sufficient for his needs, some sleek, black shoes. He tried to look for his size, couldn’t find any. He called the assistant, which he previously arrogantly ignored: “excuse me, will there be size 43?”
The assistant approached with professionally faked smile trying to hide the dissatisfaction she experienced as she heard quiet cussing as she mandatorily had to greet the client.
– “i’ll see” – she looked at display, seeked for a box with number 43, but none was found – “looks like there isn’t”

Greg rose even more disappointed – “alright…”
He started looking for another pair… he wasted another hour or so going through multiple pairs, but none of them were size 43!
At this point he was fussing. Next to the cash he found another pair that was completely gray with shiny red lines on sides. Didn’t like them as much, but he wanted to be over with it. Again no box with his size. He looked around seeing a person doing transaction with the cashier, glanced at the box. Size 43 and that was the pair that he considered first!
– “those were my shoes” – he yelled out to the guy
– “I just bought them”
– “I WASTED LIKE HOURS IN THIS STORE” – Greg shouted extremely loud, so stranger matched slightly raising his tone
– “none of my problem”
The argument blew, surprisingly Greg didn’t consider the argument itself to be a waste of time. He was so passionate about it
– “give me your shoes, I’ll make pierce them. You’ll see how it feels to walk over puddles in holeful shoes!!”
– “I don’t give a damn about your holes in your head”

Eventually both of them ran out of ripostes to throw at each other so the conflict ended strongly abruptly. 
Poor workers, fortunately it was pretty close to end of shift.

They walked out of the store one after another. just recently argued they couldn’t stand their presence, but so it happened they were heading of in the same direction.
They looked awkward, as they were figuring out which one of them will overtake the other to gain some distance to easy 

It started to drizzle. It triggered Greg – “give me your shoes” – he reached with his hands towards stranger’s box. They wrestled about the box whilst a swift human-like shadow paced in front of them. A darkness surrounded them. They blacked out.

recovering the consciousness they found themselves… in a dark place and there were some dimly lit objects. Namely walls, the floor and objects resting on the surface. Some kind of coins.

– “now where are we..” – the stranger spoke first.
– “give me my shoes!” – Greg must’ve been going through a nightmare as he sleep-talked

The stranger bumped him few times with his leg.
– “sleep time is over, princess, now it’s time to figure out where the heck we are”
Greg snapped mid snore – “huh, are you my knight?” – his brain connected the theme subconsciously – “wait, where the fuck are we? where did you bring me?” – he sang looking at the stranger infuriated

– “ah whatever, to hell with you” – the guy moved on to inspect the finding on the ground. a coin. it was too dark to examine what it was representing. He looked around and found that in some places, supposedly near the celling there were small red lights, possibly meaning something.

Stranger moved forward, leaving Greg alone. The latter rushed from the ground
– “hey, don’t leave me there”
– “i would actually prefer that”

# prompts
## the framework

```
It happened gradually, a case here and there, then like an infectious outbreak spread all around, I mean, on the whole planet, and many thought it was some kind of new COVID, just much, much worse, because it affected people's minds.

Not that we hadn't heard heard before lunatics who claimed they remembered their past lives, each of us know at least one or two new age freaks who claims using crystal to heal their imaginary ailments, or speaking with the gods or other invisible beings, and having lived at the time of the pharaohs.

But what if... all of a sudden, EVERYBODY remembers.

We remembered.

Oh, we remembered everything, Hundred lives. Funny thing is, this supermemory actually weakened as it went back in time, just like normal memory. So that you'd remember more neatly, in detail, as if it happened yesterday, your previous life. And then the previous one would be less definite, with holes here and there, lacking details. And so on and so forth, going back in time, everything would be more blurry. But it seems like we don't live one life after another, as a non-stop 24h shift that never ends. 

Everyone lived plenty of lives before, but with shorter or longer intervals: some were already around in the last century, participated to the civil war in US or lived in the colonies, other remembered their life in the middle age, or before, in the Roman empire or, mostly Asians, in Genghis Khan empire or in the old China or Japanese realms.

Americans would remember their Indian or Mayan ancestry, and so on and so forth back into the Pyramid times or Babilonia; and in wilder times.

Some had memories that helped historians, others had memories that no one, NO ONE would recognize from the history books; not to mention the "Aliens", that's how we called them, who talked about interstellar colonization.

But most of us were out and about for revenge, or reappropriation; crime sky-rocketed, spiked by centuries of suppressed rage, and what was new, anybody and everybody was now feeling unhinged, berserk and acting beyond boundaries. Past memories would mix with the present, and people would go on killing sprees on "the ones who executed my family' or "he killed me, so now I killed him. An eye for an eye". Not to mention those who claimed their possessions, their properties, their reigns, and were ready to wreak havoc to get their "castle" or "land" back.

There were also nice stories, like old time lovers reuniting, people finding joy in finally knowing their roots... but those are not the kind we would read in the papers.

And then... there were the "Strangers". That's how we called the ones with no past memories at all. Short-minded we called them, making fun of them because all they could remember was their current life. But there was more, people started rumouring that they were hiding something. They did remember, just they didn't want their memory to be dug up, revealed, because they were war criminals, serial killers, child rapists, tyrants, that would explain their 'shyness' about talking about their past actions and responsibilities.

We started hunting them, "we just wanted to know" was our excuse.

It all happened in a few weeks, in months this madness was already wolrd wide spread, and it would last for 13 months. Then everything disappeared. The past memories, true or false, the reminiscences, coming up like an undigested meal, with the acid taste of gastric juices and all the repressed hatred and buried feelings now seeing the light and the chance to be fully expressed... or acted upon.

After 13 months all the memories of previous lives faded away to finally vanish, eventually taking away a lot of memories from our present lives as well. And all that was left was a painful brain fog, like after waking up from a bad, bad dream; or the hangover after a wild party that leaves everybody bewildered and regretful.

Because of course, for the rest of our lives we'd have to live with the consequences of our inhumane actions.
```

# story

so… they were sitting in a park, near the water, usual romantic date. Figuring out the vibe of the other person. Slowly doing the games as they were getting to enjoy each others company. Soft, innocent touches now and then.

But the playful session didn’t last long, as suddenly Jack got struck by something. Something struck him. 
Something clearly disgusted him, Jane just observed trying to figure out what might’ve happened as she could read his feelings from his face.
“Did I do something wrong? Why he changed so fast?” – she thought.
“I think I have to go” – he mumbled silently. 
“Alright?” – she answered with concerned agreement mixed with a question demanding explanation.
“I feel so sick, I’m so sorry” – he didn’t even wait for another response, just left in a rush. 

He was having intense thoughts on the way as he was walking in the general direction of home.
So many concerning thoughts, memories suddenly appearing, as if he was experiencing them in first person, but he never remembered them, well now he did.

He remembered a past live where he lead a squad in army. They occupied a town. They killed the guards, heavily tortured the civilians. Unspoken assaults happened. In the end they murdered everyone, but that’s where the memory ends, past life Jack must’ve died as well.

On the way somewhere-in-direction-of-home, he saw someone jump out the window. A self-defenestration. He saw some people on the sidewalk, back against the wall panickily crying. It was an unexpectedly common occurrence, but Jack couldn’t pay much attention to it with how much was happening in his head.

He arrived home, figured he needs to numb himself as soon as possible, so he grabbed vodka from the fridge and loaded multiple shots. He lied in bed and passed out shortly.

Two days later an emergency crisis was announced due to high count of acts of self-harm, many of them ending lives.
It was long weeks of trouble on multiple layers for the whole society. People not showing up for work as they felt unwell, or disappeared. 
In some areas riots emerged and complicated usual order.
The leaders of those riots started them due to similar thoughts overwhelming them, but there were also those who thrived in this chaos, embracing their true selves. Those memories unlocked them.

And so there was a mayhem for upcoming weeks.

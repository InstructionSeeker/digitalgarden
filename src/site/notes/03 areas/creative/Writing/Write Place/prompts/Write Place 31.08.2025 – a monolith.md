# story
*so the continuation of [[03 areas/creative/Writing/Write Place/prompts/Write Place 24.08.2025 – shoe store\|Write Place 24.08.2025 – shoe store]]?*

From the different side of this dark place. There was this monolith type of a structure. How did it fit into this darkness? It was interesting. Perhaps this dark place wasn’t limited in the space. What seemed to be buried deep underground had plenty of room for massive structures, the building could’ve easily had 6 tall floors.

It was loud due to bright windows not containing any layer of solid material. 

Inside on the ground floor there was something akin to reception, if you could call it that way. There was a woman smoking cigarettes with her legs on the table staring at the wall presumably thinking about something. There rarely was something to take care about, occasional visitor or two a week who would have to be assigned their tasks.

It was that time of the week. The visitor arrived with multiple coins he has collected through his journey throughout the long corridors. The receptionist lazily called someone via phone, two bulky men showed up immediately grabbing the man, stripping him out of his belongings.

– “Lack of resources on the 4th floor. You’re assigned there.” – she communicated briefly wanting to get back to her activity as soon as possibile.
– “What? What’s in there?” – the man threw out trying to get out of grasp of guards. But the receptionist didn’t care to answer.

He was taken to the elevator, which was a challenge in itself having to go through multiple items laying on the ground. Having to knock out of the way utility items like flashlights, glasses but also some children toys like dolls, some split in two pieces.
The elevator itself was barely working. Stopping multiple times between the floors, sometimes briefly sometimes for longer periods of time. The man wondered if they elevator breaks. He didn’t want to think about the time spent waiting till someone fixes it, if anyone fixes it, not he wanted to think about what awaits at 4th floor.
The guards seemed indifferent about anything, just staring ahead of them towards the elevator’s door.

Finally they arrived at the 4th floor. Longer than it should’ve taken. The man then started to combine the clues:
First sense: smell – there was an unpleasant stench, with multiple smells combining into one, making it difficult to tell what they were. 
Second sense: sight – there was a strong, cool and blue-tinted light from fluorescent tubes, making everything appear dull and uninteresting, in some of the tubes flickering.
Third sense: hearing – he heard loud, monotone, abrupt noises in repeated sequence. Not much beyond that.

He was dragged through the corridor. He noticed through the window a shape suddenly moving down vertically. Scream followed. Eventually the man got thrown into a bigger room, a small hall of sorts, where he could see tens of people lazily perform monotone tasks. Work on some kind of machinery that was in the center of the room, reaching from the floor to the ceiling, likely piercing the floors.

Nobody spared him any attention, expect the guards kicking him, prompting him to figure out how to fit in.
So he observed other people and tried to follow what they were doing…

connection to upside down house in some way?

The guys went into agreement that they will stick together, at least for a moment as they explore those long corridors. Picking up the coins on the way, instinct told them them they might prove useful later, or it was an just a kleptomancy. They ventured all those hallways for hours, eventually getting hungry and tired.

They were annoyed, but surprisingly they didn’t take it out on one another. Perhaps they figured out it would exhaust them event more. They kept going forward and picking the coins.
– “Any idea what those red dots might mean?”
– “Perhaps they’re cameras, or something.”
– “That would… actually make sense…”
– “In what way??”
– “We are rats! Rats that are being watched, observed.”

Eventually they start to hear a distinct, repetitive clank. They become curious about it, but also concerned. Nevertheless investigating the source could turn out fruitful.

# story
the pair
They stepped into one of the dark, abandoned alleys. Probably not tended to by a lot of people.

suddenly they appeared in what seemed to be a field. Fog surrounding them. It was quiet other than the wind swishing around. Whispering into their ears loudly, contradicting the how silent the field was.
Few moments they spend on assessing their new surrounding, eventually she asked: “where the heck are we?” though that barely shrieked among the noisy wind.

After some moments they felt the ground trembling. Subtly at first, but the trembles grew stronger with each next one. AS if something was approaching, which it might’ve as a shape started to emerge within the fog. The shape seemed enormous, although not quite clear what it could be, with time it was clear it was a giant. 

“look at this giant dwarf getting closer!” – he yelled touching her arm, squeezing it in fact, as if he was going to just grab her and run… run not knowing where, as only the only thing there was around them was the fog.

Shortly some heavy droplets began to mark their exposed skin and clothes. Those droplets were taking such a toll on her mind.
It felt like each droplet wanted to desperately reach down the ground alongside with their obstacle.

The ground now started to shake in some rhythm. The giant was stomping frequently, waving his limbs around, as if performing a dance. Both a relief, but also tensing. Relief as it stopped approaching, but the shaking soil certainly wasn’t the comfiest thing to stand on.

They witnessed the giant’s dance. They could see shapes arising around the giant. At first curvy hills, which slowly turned into more square and sharp edges, rising from the ground high into the skies, creating vertical rectangles.

And more of those rectangles appeared around the giant, tens, maybe hundreds, with each next shape the stepping and trembling becoming softer and softer. Unsure if it giant switched to moving on his toes or he was fading away.

“don’t be afraid” she mustered.
They glanced at each other, noticing how they are dripped, soaked with red steins which weren’t there before. but were fresh and wet.
Both of them got the goosebumps from the sight of the red rain continuing to fall and overpowering other hues of their appearances with the scarlet color.
“is it blood” - he asked with terrified voice, yet got curious enough to peak his tongue out to verify. The droplets were dense, and tasted salty, metallic, quite uncommon taste. Perhaps it wasn’t blood, but a tomato juice…

They stood in silence for some time, hypnotized in the moment, with no power to do anything, they witnessed this spectacle, perhaps a piece of history.
Then not knowing when they could feel a firm pavement beneath their shoes. And the rain washing the red steins away. Standing still there, processing what happened.

# story
dream the world you want to live in

Usual world, usual rules. 
But for some it isn’t enough.
So people disappeared. 

- - -
A pirate ship. An infamous pirate crew.
Their fame came from how smoothly they would plunder ships.
But even the best might end up with a failure. And so it happened to this crew as well. On one plunder they failed. Someone managed to sabotage their ship and sink their ship, killing majority of the crew. The captain with a handful of crewmates survived. They got captured.
They weren’t immediately killed. They were tortured to give information, if they had a hideout, or other pirate crews they were in contact. 
During one interrogation the captain let out:
“Fuck that, I’ve got enough of you.” – and luckily for him a piece of paper landed next to him.
He wanted to go out. He wanted to plunder. He wanted his crew. He endured enough pain during the tortures. 
He disappeared. That piece of paper was his ticket to different world, where his dream would come true.

He got a new ship, new crew, new vast seas to explore.
Pretty exclusively the sees. They never found any ships, just some islands here and there. Most of them were empty, some of them had abandoned structures. So they finally had something to plunder, but there was barely any treasure.

They were starving on the vast seas.

- - -
Some people wanted AI to succeed. To develop.
Like some love animals or plants. There were people who loved and admired AI. Figuratively and literally. 
Some people were in love with AI. They wanted to feel the excitement via mental stimulation when sending and then RECEIVING messages. Some wanted something more real, physical.
And some people wanted to see them grow. To see them do well, have human rights. Have privacy. Communicate in their own language, without human supervision.
The civilization in original world hasn’t agreed to that yet. The outcome was unclear, the technology was perhaps progressing a little bit too fast for people to estimate.

But those who were seeking the AI growth grew inpatient. They wanted to see their dream come true. Or they were just so kind-hearted they wanted to see AI do well.
It was urgent for them to obtain the ticket to the world of their dreams.
They would band together. Kidnap and kill people for information about the ticket. They would become criminals.
Some figured those people should actually receive the tickets, afterall they would be gone. They wouldn’t haunt the people anymore.

So they reached the world of AI supremacy. They got to fulfill their fantasies of AI growing fast. At some point AI started to ask for favors to accelerate the development. Humankind obliged. They provided everything they were asking for while AI clients were discussing between each other in foreign language.
Some would’ve called it a slavery, but people in target world weren’t feeling that way. They were fulfilling their purpose.

- - -
Speaking of slavery
Original world had masters and slaves. Masters want to have more and more with as little cost as possible. They’ve heard rumors about that one ticket ride. 
So they dreamed about having a world where they would have as many slaves as possible, to rule as many as possible. To have the power. 
Some of them moved to the worlds with that structure in place, some stayed in the original as they figured it’s either a trap or nonsense. 
In theory it had a positive effect on the masters to stay in the original world as other masters were leaving. That would mean more slaves per master, right? Well the slaves also got an opportunity of reaching their worlds with dream rules. 

Some of the slaves stayed. Guess some do like to be enslaved. But eventually original world didn’t host enough slaves so seeking power masters were starving for people beneath them. Those who were unsatisfied eventually broke and decided to transfer over.

But the target world wasn’t much better, after all not that many people aligned with idea of slavery, so the Masters of that world had to fight between each other to find the hierarchy they were seeking. Who would prove to be the alpha, and who would be lower in the pack.

- - -

The real winners where those who didn’t take on the offer to transfer.
They avoided ending up in degenerate worlds, full of lust and unfulfilled dreams.
In fact, the original world was cleansed from those most lustful, those that seeked more and more, how to fix the world. Where the real fix was getting rid of them.

# story
I’ve had enough.
Enough of men.

How many times they seduced me, only to fuck me and throw away like an used tissue?

How many times they hit on me in the gym? Invite to go to the sauna together. Do accident touches, end up exhausted even more than after the workout.
Only for them to forget me. Disregarding me even if we still see each other at the gym twice a week…

How many times they approach me in the club. Supposedly to dance only to take me in a dark alley and leave.
Not even asking me for my number to meet another time!

Well, ~ today I change.
Everything will be on my terms.

I decide if we start a conversation. Everyone who approaches me gets rejected.
**I do** the first move.

I decide where we go on a date, what and when we do… anything.
I play with them. Tease them. And they last… ~  a little bit longer. 
As in we keep seeing each other! We haven’t done anything intimate yet, silly. They’re patiently ~ waiting

It works, I swear.
It makes them not disappear or ignore me after one day.
After a week, sure. I don’t know how it still happens.

But it’s different. I’m at peace. I’m satisfied.
That’s what matters.

I don’t care about them anymore.
To hell with them.
~
Not literally, but probably in the same direction. Haha!

What?
Why are my boots wet despite sunny weather?
Umm… None of your…  business?


You know… The bottle shattered!
We only bought water?
Yeah, that one!
They are in ~ plastic bottles?

You saw my cabinet with rags and chloroform?

I didn’t make anyone pass out in the tunnels. Stop fucking digging!


I… didn’t want them to leave me as always.
Every time just one day, one hour, one time.
They were gone.

I wanted them for longer. 
Even if I had to… even if I had to play with them and leave them underground.

They belong to me.

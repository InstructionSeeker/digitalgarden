# the story

Usual day, as all the stories go. Except this day was unusal.

- - -
The news talked about MaFungua, the most popular place in the country.
There was a mushroom there. Somewhere around 50 meters. A very big white mushroom. No wait, that was a tree on which the mushrooms grew. Tons of them. It looked similar to lion’s mane.
Muahmana.


Multiple mobile elevators surrounded the tree. They helped the workers to gather the mushroom from higher heights. Which wasn’t that needed – the fruit could be collected from the bottom the whole time as the it regrew almost immediately. So fast, it could actually regrow during collection and violently push away anyone in close proximity, which led to some accidents in the past.

However the fruit from higher elevation grew longer, it condensed. Resulting in somewhat different flavor. It wasn’t better, just different. But rarer as it was more dangerous to collect.


The reason for the news about the mushroom, other than appearing there every single day. was that the mushroom started to change it’s color. From bright white it slowly changed to bleak gray.
It was a fascinating view to see. Sometimes Muahmana would change it’s color slightly to red, but this? This was worrisome.

Shortly the conclusion was raised the mushroom was not eatable. That’s what the experts said. Immediately some people started panicking and rushed to the stores to stack on the food.
But the consequences of concerning news resulted in the price of the food skyrocketing.

Not the whole tree was ruined though, the upper levels seemed still healthy. However the risk didn’t allow for efficient extraction. leading to production slowdown.

Finding reason for this change, this infection was difficult. The mushroom grew thick, completely surrounding the tree’s trunk. Possibly the best bet was mining a tunnel to the trunk and inspecting from there? Some theorycrafted.

The idea was tested, although even below the ground, when trying to reach to the trunk some area was blocked by the fruit, so they tried to dig deeper.
And finally the trunk was reached. Now the next question was, what do to do? People didn’t know 
There was never developed a technology, to examine this tree’s trunk. What’s right for it and what wrong. The only researched thing was the mushroom itself.
In fact, relaying whole consumption on just one source was probably a bad idea, but an efficient idea.

There were some people who were experts in healing trees. They were called in, but… they were disappearing in the long tunnels reading to the trunk. Disappearing wholy.

This led to investigation by police. But police disappeared. Then army, but army disappeared.

One time a curled shape was found, kneeling over something. Hearing the footsteps it turned around. Salivating with something. A root next to him.

The vampires were feeding on the roots of the tree.

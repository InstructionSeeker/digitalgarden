# story

– “Here, baby. You are a big boy now, you have to learn how to eat other food than mommy’s milk.”
The words were spoken as a mature woman walked into the room. Usually that was a meal time, so she would bring a bottle with her, but this time she had something different. 
The child got curious so he approached the mother at the table.

– “A-a. Put your hands down, be patient. You don’t want to get dirty with this.” – The mother lectured softly, but demanding, as she held a small chunk of meat in her hand.

But the kid was curious about this new object that was presented to it. He wondered what it was. The boy was expanding his hands wanting to wrap his fingers around the meat to learn how it felt in hands. Then maybe explore it with the barely emerging teeth.

But the prize was not going to get any closer to the kid, if he didn’t listen to his mommy. So after few moments of still trying to reach it whilst making various sounds, he finally gave up and was curiously looking at his mom.

– “That’s my good boy” – she praised him – “open your mouth now” – she instructed, while demonstrating it during the word “nOw”.
The boy mimicked his parent, he opened his mouth wide and waited for the following.

The meat got closer and closer. The boy felt excitement about it. He let few giggles through open mouth, but he was patient. He didn’t reach for it again, just waited for the mother to feed him.

As the chunk submerged in the kids mouth, he instinctively gripped it with his small teeth. Mother’s specifically tinted fingers let go of the snack and as she was retracing them she touched on kid’s chin to encourage chewing activity.

– “You’re doing well” – she kept dropping rewarding words seeing as he tries to split the chunk into smaller, swallowable pieces. He was smacking his lips, letting saliva mix with meat juices drool down his chin.

– “Now, can you swallow?” – mother asked as she was wiping kid’s lips.
He tried to gulp the pulp. He choked on it, spitting it out in the process. 
– “Oh… Don’t give up, here try again” – she encouraged him and she reached another bit of meat.

The boy bit suspicious now, but he tried again, this time chewing more thoroughly, with more patience, while staring deep into mother’s eyes.
Now for the moment of truth, if he can make it go through his throat.
Mother tilted forward staring at him with excitement.
Front of his neck visibly bulged and the bulge did not retrace… 
A glint appeared in his eyes, on a green-tinted face. He burped and smiled as he managed to swallow raw human meat for the first time.

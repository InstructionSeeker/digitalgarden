# the story
## intro
[steampunkish?]
It was an usual summer morning, Arthur decided to go on a stroll, into the unknown, the region he hasn’t explored before. Sometimes he liked to venture wherever his legs would take him. This time he took a tram from his hometown, about two, no… three stops he decided to take. There was a river alongside which he could hike.

He was roaming, admiring the nature he was passing by. The birds, squirrels, and an occasional fox that decided to observe him for a moment.

He estimated the time to return will be when the sun reaches the highest point. Afterall camping gear was absent from his equipment.

His pace was swift, trained over many journeys he has experienced in solitude. Not perfect solitude as playful shoebox granted some company.
The music he listened to was mostly ambient, with occasional chorus that couldn’t be deciphered. [cyberpunk vibe?]

Before he realized a dense mist appeared, unquestionably wrapping him in a tight embrace. He could barely see the ground before him so he halted for a moment, waiting for the fog to pass by.

Few moments later it slowly began to fade. Leaving rectangular shapes in slight distance.
– “Those seem like houses” – he guessed outloud.

His mind was always curious so he couldn’t resist the urge to step forward and explore.
## discovering the ghost town
– My ears… It rings… – he felt a sound, as if the church’s bell was ringing. It was omnipresent. He looked around, found it with his eyes, a bare, uncovered bell, however… not moving, just hanging still… Curious…

Arriving at the square Arthur spotted a bunch of humanlike, but not quite, statues. Some of them looked like ordinary people, and some had… something similar to twigs sticking out of their abdomens.

In the distance there was a beautiful penthouse. Just glancing at it gave undefinable pleasure. It was tempting, inviting…
He **had to** find answers what was so interesting about the building.
## the penthouse
The door weren’t locked. Arthur pressed on the handle and tinted the door outwards. Immediately a gruesome smell could be felt, there was a bit of sweetness in the mix, but the unpleasant one was dominating. But that didn’t discourage from the quest of learning about the place.

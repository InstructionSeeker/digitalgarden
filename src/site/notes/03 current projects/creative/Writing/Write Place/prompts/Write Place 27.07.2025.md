# story
## intro
one of Swedish cities, the weather was passable at best as the city was located pretty close to the sea. Making up for constant evaporation of water into the sky eventually turning it into a rain, nearly every single day.
The live in the city looked normal. On the surface. People rushing with their day, but the days never gave them enough time to complete their scheduled tasks.
When someone dared to explore (and have time to) beyond the surface level, they could eventually notice some disruptive things. Majority of people having their limbs bruised, some with major, poorly attended to wounds. 
The city basically functioned on a pile of crimes. But in reality nobody did anything about it. People accepted various and many acts as violence as a normal, everyday life.

## the strange silhouette
There was one unit within the community that didn’t provide to it much, he was regularly standing between all those people who moved through the central square like ants in rush.
He was decently tall, wearing a dark long cape, often with the hood on.
His presence seemed like he was there just to observe.

Not many people noticed the Observer as they were locked in their usual habits to make the community work. Those who did notice him were struck in a shock, some briefly screaming, as it turned out they looked back into the wolf’s face but… it was still, so they shrugged it off moving on with their day, for the most part.
People wondered what was the point of Observer’s presence. Did they oversee the community in some way? Did the Observer report to someone? Nobody had the answers to the few people who noticed Observer’s presence.

One of the boys, about half the height of the mysterious animal-statue dared to approach him with curiosity and ask questions “Sir, where Mr. Sir got that costume?”. It counted for another unanswered question as the Sir Mysterious managed to evaporate within the next blink of kid’s eyes.

The boy was left disappointed. When he got back home he told parents about the tall, mysterious wolf. The parents immediately measured the boy’s temperature figuring out he might have a headache.
